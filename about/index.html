About

Wassim Ait Oubahou is a mechanical engineer focused on structural analysis, spacecraft mechanical systems, composite structures and mechanical design. He holds an MEng in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Derby.

Beyond spacecraft structures, his broader engineering interests extend to robotic and unmanned systems and the mechanical infrastructure required for future space logistics.
